# Budget Buddy 3.1: Budster gets a calendar and a magnifying glass

Budster has been busy. It turns out a monthly limit is lovely, but it doesn't tell you that the concert tickets quietly ate the whole fun budget. And typing in the rent every single month is nobody's idea of a good time. So 3.1 fixes both.

Same two backpacks as before:

- **Budget-Buddy-OCR-v3.1.apk** reads receipts on the phone and suggests the details for you.
- **Budget-Buddy-No-OCR-v3.1.apk** keeps entry manual and stays nice and small.

Both update straight over 3.0 and keep everything you've saved.

## What's new

- **Category limits on Analytics.** A new card shows every category that has its own budget: how much you've spent out of its limit, a bar in your gauge colours, and a word from Budster. "Plenty of wiggle room!" at one end, "Over by €15.00. Budster needs a little lie-down." at the other.
- **Repeating transactions.** Rent, salary, subscriptions, the gym you swear you'll go to: set them to repeat every week, month or year, right under the date. When the day comes round, Budster adds the record for you and says so. If you set one up with an old start date, the missed ones are filled in straight away.
- **Fair and square copies.** A repeated record doesn't copy your receipt photo, and if you delete one, Budster won't sneak it back in. Monthly repeats on the 31st land on the last day of shorter months and go back to the 31st afterwards.
- **A spending chart you can actually read.** Every category now gets its own bar and label, biggest spender at the top, with the amount on the end. Bars use the same colours as the Category limits card, and categories without a limit wear Budget Buddy teal. "Show on Graph" now stacks what you've spent against what's left of each limit, without the numbers piling up.

## Little things Budster tidied up

- The spending-limit amount on Analytics was losing its last digit on narrow screens. It's whole again.
- Budster's mood line on Analytics no longer gets cut off halfway through, and it says "15%" instead of "15%%".

## Before you install

- Android 8.1 or newer is required, same as before.
- 3.1 installs over 3.0 and keeps your profile, records, budgets and achievements. Keep the same edition when you update.
- Camera permission is still optional, and the app still has no internet access.
- Install only the APKs attached to this release.
